Smart photo resizer

Drop in a photo and get four social media sizes, cropped to keep the subject in frame. Your photo stays on your computer: everything runs right in your browser.

Cropping:

How it works

The tool shrinks a copy of your photo and scores every pixel for detail, skin tones, and color, which is where people and subjects usually are. It then slides each crop shape across the photo to find the spot that keeps the most of that detail, with a slight preference for the center.

If a crop would cut off too much, like text on a flyer, it fits the whole photo onto a blurred copy of itself instead. I first built this in Python, then rebuilt it in JavaScript so you can try it here.
