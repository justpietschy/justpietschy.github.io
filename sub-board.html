The story behind this project

As Group Fitness Manager at Zenergy, I coordinated subs for 60+ instructors. Requests went out through email chains and group chats, and coverage was tracked across nine separate spreadsheets, so it was hard to tell who had already said yes.

I combined everything into one Google Sheet: a tab for each class family, a list of who was qualified to teach each format, and a status column so nothing got missed when updating the booking system. Instructors spent less time on email and thanked me for it.

The studio has since moved to a dedicated scheduling platform. This prototype is my take on the next step for my own system: replacing the email blast by showing each request only to the people who can actually cover it.
